About Arcshell

Home Interiors Designed Around the Way You Live

Arcshell Interiors is an interior design and modular kitchen company based in Thiruvalla, serving homes across Thiruvalla and Pathanamthitta. We handle the full scope of a home's interiors, living room, dining room, bedrooms and kitchen, along with the finishing touches that tie a space together: ceilings, wall textures, curtains and custom furniture.

Every project starts with a free site visit, so you know the scope and the quote before any work begins.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide interior design services in Pathanamthitta?

Yes. Arcshell Interiors serves both Thiruvalla and Pathanamthitta district. We design living rooms, bedrooms, dining rooms and modular kitchens for homes across the area.

What does a modular kitchen from Arcshell Interiors include?

A modular kitchen from us includes design, cabinetry, countertops and fittings, fabricated to your kitchen's exact measurements. We handle everything from planning to installation.

Do you offer a free site visit before quoting?

Yes. Every project starts with a free site visit, so we can understand the space and give you an accurate quote before any work begins.

What interior services do you offer besides the kitchen?

Beyond modular kitchens, we handle living room, dining room and bedroom interiors, along with gypsum ceilings, wall textures, wallpaper, curtain work and customized furniture.
